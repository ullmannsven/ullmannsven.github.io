I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the [University of Stuttgart](https://www.ians.uni-stuttgart.de/) and the [University of Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/MathematicsMuenster/), supervised by [Prof. Dr. Bernard Haasdonk](https://www.ians.uni-stuttgart.de/institute/team/Haasdonk-00002/) and [Prof. Dr. Mario Ohlberger](https://www.uni-muenster.de/AMM/en/ohlberger/team/mario_ohlberger.shtml).

My research is in surrogate modeling and model order reduction (MOR), with a focus on understanding structure-preserving and nonlinear MOR approaches. This work brings together several areas: it is rooted in numerical analysis and scientific computing and connects to problems from physics and engineering. In this context, I also collaborate with [Prof. Dr.-Ing. Jörg Fehr](https://www.itm.uni-stuttgart.de/en/institute/team/Fehr/) from the Institute of Engineering and Computational Mechanics at the University of Stuttgart.

Besides my research, I am one of the main developers of [pyMOR](https://pymor.org), an open-source Python library for model order reduction.

Research interests
======
* Model order reduction
* Structure preserving surrogate modeling 
* Kernel methods
* PDE-constrained optimization
* Machine learning for scientific computing
