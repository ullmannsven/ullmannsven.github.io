I am a PhD student in Mathematics at [Mathematics Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/MathematicsMuenster/) and the [University of Stuttgart](https://www.ians.uni-stuttgart.de/). My research lies at the intersection of numerical analysis, scientific computing and data-driven modeling. I develop methods that make expensive simulations, typically governed by partial differential equations, fast enough for many-query tasks such as optimization, control and uncertainty quantification.

Research interests
======
* **Model order reduction**: reduced basis methods and structure-preserving reduction, in particular for port-Hamiltonian and coupled systems
* **Kernel methods and surrogate modeling**: Hermite kernel interpolation and greedy kernel approximation
* **PDE-constrained optimization**: trust-region methods with certified surrogate models
* **Machine learning for scientific computing**: e.g. (group-)convolutional autoencoders for nonlinear model reduction

Software
======
I am a contributor to [pyMOR](https://pymor.org), an open-source Python library for model order reduction. The code for my research projects is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ullmannsven).
