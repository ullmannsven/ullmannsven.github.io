I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the [University of Stuttgart](https://www.ians.uni-stuttgart.de/) and the [University of Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/MathematicsMuenster/), supervised by [Prof. Dr. Bernard Haasdonk](https://www.ians.uni-stuttgart.de/institute/team/Haasdonk-00002/) and [Prof. Dr. Mario Ohlberger](https://www.uni-muenster.de/AMM/en/ohlberger/team/mario_ohlberger.shtml). My research lies at the intersection of numerical analysis, scientific computing and data-driven modeling. I develop methods that make expensive simulations fast enough for many-query tasks such as optimization and control.

I am also one of the main developers of [pyMOR](https://pymor.org), an open-source Python library for model order reduction.

Research interests
======
* Model order reduction
* Kernel methods and surrogate modeling
* PDE-constrained optimization
* Machine learning for scientific computing
